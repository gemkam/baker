Read First

Please read this before you order

  1. Order time. Brownies, cupcakes, cookies and biscuits: at least 2 days before. All cakes: at least 3 days before.
  2. How to order. Tap a box size or cake weight to add it to your order, then send your order on WhatsApp.
  3. Payment. Brownies, cupcakes, cookies and biscuits are paid 100% in advance. Cakes need a 50% advance to book your date, and the balance is paid before pickup or delivery.
  4. Order confirmation. Your order is confirmed only after the advance is received. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp.
  5. Cake photo approval. When your cake is ready, we send you a photo on WhatsApp. Once you approve it and pay the balance, the cake is handed over.
  6. Changes and cancellation. Changes to flavour, theme or message are accepted up to 2 days before your date. The advance is non-refundable once baking has started.
  7. Quality check. Please check your order at pickup or delivery. If there is any problem, send us a photo within 2 hours and before eating. We will replace the item or give a discount on your next order. No refunds after the items are eaten.
  8. Allergies and diet. When you order, you choose egg or eggless, with dairy or dairy-free, regular or gluten-free, and nuts or no nuts, and you can name any nut you are allergic to. We will confirm on WhatsApp if your items can be made that way. Everything is homemade in a kitchen that also uses eggs, dairy, wheat and nuts, so we cannot promise zero traces.
